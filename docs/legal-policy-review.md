## Operational review before production

- Confirm the registered legal entity/controller name and privacy contact. The public text currently identifies the existing VitalReach brand and published contact address, not an invented corporate entity.
- Adopt record-specific retention periods, backup expiry, and an identity-verification and access/correction/deletion procedure. The application currently has no automatic clinical-record deletion deadline; the notice says so explicitly.
- Confirm actual hosting/processor locations, contracts and any required international-transfer safeguards.
- Have the responsible clinical/privacy adviser assess Ghana and relevant US/Maryland obligations, regulatory registration and incident-notification duties. No HIPAA certification or blanket compliance claim is made by these pages.
- Ensure staff can honor the stated restrictions: no health-data marketing, no sale of personal information, and separate clinical permissions. Review these notices before adding newsletters, advertising trackers, new providers or new health-data uses.
- Existing general commercial clauses remain; have the business owner confirm applicable seller identity, shipping/refund arrangements and governing-law wording before production.
